The studio

A studio led by makers and listeners.

Lumière is a small, hands-on practice working between Lisbon and the coast. We believe a room should hold its calm — that good design disappears into the background of a life well lived.

Every project starts with a conversation and a sketch, never a moodboard downloaded from the internet.

  • Listen first — the brief always comes before the moodboard.
  • Material honesty — we choose what ages well, not what photographs well.
  • Light as a material — the first thing we design with is the day itself.
